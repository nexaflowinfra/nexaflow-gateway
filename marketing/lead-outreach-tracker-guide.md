# NexaFlow Lead Outreach Tracker Guide

Use `lead-outreach-tracker.csv` to record target merchants, outreach status, and follow-up actions for NexaFlow Enquiry.

## Best-fit merchant targets

Prioritize local service businesses that receive enquiries through WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram, Google Business Profile, or phone calls.

Good early targets:
- Renovation and interior design
- Beauty salons and aesthetic clinics
- Tuition and enrichment centres
- Cleaning, repair, and maintenance services
- Event, bridal, and photography services
- Small agencies and local consultants

## Field guide

- `Business Name`: Merchant or company name.
- `Industry`: Their business type, such as renovation, beauty, tuition, cleaning, or clinic.
- `Country/City`: Location for local targeting.
- `Website or Social Page`: Public website, Facebook page, Instagram profile, LinkedIn page, or Google Business listing.
- `Public Email`: Only use business emails published publicly by the merchant.
- `WhatsApp/Phone`: Public business WhatsApp or phone number.
- `Contact Source`: Where you found the lead, such as Google Maps, LinkedIn, Facebook, referral, or existing network.
- `Contact Person`: Owner, manager, admin, or unknown.
- `Fit Score (1-5)`: 5 means very likely to need enquiry tracking and fast WhatsApp follow-up.
- `Why They May Need NexaFlow`: One sentence about their likely pain point.
- `First Message Angle`: The opening reason you will use, such as missed enquiries, faster replies, trial offer, or organized follow-up.
- `Outreach Channel`: Email, WhatsApp, LinkedIn, phone, referral, or in-person.
- `Consent/Source Notes`: Record why it is okay to contact them, for example public business email on website.
- `Status`: Current stage.
- `Last Contacted`: Date of your last message.
- `Next Follow-up`: Date to follow up.
- `Trial Interest`: Yes, No, Maybe, or Unknown.
- `Trial Started`: Date trial starts, if applicable.
- `Plan Interest`: Trial, Starter, Pro, Business, or Unknown.
- `Notes`: Conversation details and next action.

## Recommended statuses

Use these exact status names so the tracker stays clean:

- `Not contacted`
- `Connection requested`
- `Message sent`
- `Replied`
- `Interested`
- `Trial offered`
- `Trial started`
- `Follow-up scheduled`
- `Not interested`
- `Do not contact`
- `Converted`

## Outreach rules

- Contact only public business channels or people you already have a legitimate relationship with.
- Do not use scraped personal emails or private phone numbers.
- Do not send the same bulk message to everyone. Personalize the first line for each merchant.
- If a merchant says no, mark `Do not contact` and stop.
- Keep the message short: pain point, value, trial offer, and one simple question.

## Daily workflow

1. Add 10 target merchants to the CSV.
2. Score each merchant from 1 to 5.
3. Contact only the strongest 5 to 10 leads first.
4. Set `Next Follow-up` 3 to 5 days later.
5. Move interested merchants to `Trial offered` or `Trial started`.
6. Review which industry replies the most, then focus future outreach there.

## Simple first message angle

Use this as the base idea, then personalize it:

> Hi, I noticed many local service businesses receive enquiries from WhatsApp and social media but often lose track of follow-ups. NexaFlow helps collect enquiries into one private inbox, prepares a WhatsApp reply draft, and keeps customer follow-up organized. We are offering a 1-month trial for selected local merchants. Would you like me to show you a simple demo?
