# NotebookLM Podcast Prompt - English Version

Copy this prompt into NotebookLM Video Overview or Audio Overview.

```text
Create a two-person podcast-style promotional video or audio for NexaFlow.

Language:
English.

Length:
45 to 60 seconds.

Format:
Two-person podcast conversation. Natural Q&A. One host asks common business questions, the other host explains clearly.

Target audience:
Local service business owners who receive enquiries through WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram, Google Business Profile, referrals, or their website.

Visual style if generating video:
Use the uploaded NexaFlow brand style as reference. Premium dark background, deep teal technology lines, warm gold highlights, modern SaaS feel, clean business ecosystem theme. Avoid cartoon, childish, overly colorful, or playful visuals.

Core positioning:
NexaFlow Enquiry is an AI WhatsApp enquiry assistant for local service businesses. It helps merchants stop losing customer enquiries by turning scattered messages into an organized follow-up inbox.

Conversation structure:
Host A opens with the common problem:
"A lot of small businesses get enquiries from WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, and Google, but many leads are lost because nobody tracks them properly."

Host B explains:
"That is where NexaFlow Enquiry comes in. The merchant gets one simple enquiry link. Customers submit their name, phone number, message, and consent. NexaFlow organizes everything in a private inbox."

Host A asks:
"Does the business need a website?"

Host B answers:
"No. The merchant can share the link directly on WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram, Google Business Profile, or add it to a website later."

Host A asks:
"What does the AI actually do?"

Host B answers:
"It helps identify urgent enquiries, service type, possible customer intent, and prepares a WhatsApp reply draft so the team can follow up faster."

Host A asks:
"Who is this best for?"

Host B answers:
"Service businesses like renovation, cleaning, repair, beauty, education, clinic, events, and any business that depends on customer enquiries."

Host A asks:
"Why start now?"

Host B answers:
"Because most lost leads do not look like lost leads. They just sit inside chat history. NexaFlow helps make every enquiry visible, organized, and easier to follow up."

Final call to action:
"Start with a 30-day trial of NexaFlow Enquiry and see if it helps your business respond faster."

Mention the ecosystem briefly:
NexaFlow starts with Enquiry and can later grow into CRM, Billing, Inventory, and Automation from one shared business core.

Avoid:
Do not mention API, token, model routing, OpenAI, OpenRouter, webhook, database, backend, Railway, Cloudflare, Stripe, Resend, or infrastructure.
Do not guarantee sales or revenue.
Do not say the system replaces the business owner.
Do not overpromise.
Keep it simple, practical, and trustworthy.
```
