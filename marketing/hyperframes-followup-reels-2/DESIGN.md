# NexaFlow Follow-Up Pain Reels Design

## Style Prompt

Dark, premium AI business interface for local service merchants. Use NexaFlow's black/teal/gold identity with restrained glow, clean cards, flowing lines, and high-contrast bilingual subtitles. The video should feel practical and business-focused, not flashy or abstract.

## Colors

- Background: `#030504`
- Panel: `#0c0f0e`
- Text: `#f8faf7`
- Muted text: `#b9b3a5`
- Teal accent: `#2dd4bf`
- Gold accent: `#f3c76a`
- Warning red: `#ff6b6b`

## Typography

- Primary: `Inter`, `Segoe UI`, `Arial`, sans-serif
- Chinese fallback: `Microsoft YaHei`, `Noto Sans SC`, sans-serif

## Motion

- Quick but readable vertical social-video rhythm.
- Cards should slide in from small offsets, not fly wildly.
- Use subtle glow pulses, line sweeps, and card stacking.
- Every scene must work without audio because social viewers may watch muted.

## What NOT To Do

- Do not use rainbow gradients or generic SaaS blue.
- Do not overcrowd the frame with long paragraphs.
- Do not overlap subtitles with cards or CTA.
- Do not use tiny text that fails on mobile.
- Do not make the video feel like a technical API product; it is for local service merchants.
