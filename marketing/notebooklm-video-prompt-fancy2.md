# NotebookLM Video Prompt - NexaFlow Fancy 2 Brand Direction

Copy this prompt into NotebookLM after uploading the NexaFlow product brief and brand image.

```text
Please generate a short promotional explainer video or audio overview for NexaFlow.

Target audience:
Local service business owners who receive customer enquiries through WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram, Google Business Profile, or their website.

Brand direction:
Use a premium dark teal and warm gold technology style. NexaFlow should feel like a business ecosystem, not just one small tool. The first product is NexaFlow Enquiry, but the wider platform can later include CRM, Billing, Inventory, and Automation.

Length:
Under 60 seconds.

Format:
Two-person natural Q&A conversation.

Tone:
Simple, confident, premium, and easy for non-technical business owners to understand. Avoid technical language.

Core message:
NexaFlow helps local service businesses stop losing customer enquiries. It starts with an AI WhatsApp enquiry assistant, then grows into one shared business system for enquiries, CRM, billing, inventory, and automation.

Explain the product in simple steps:
1. The merchant starts with a 30-day trial.
2. NexaFlow gives the merchant one customer enquiry link.
3. The merchant shares the link on WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram, Google Business Profile, or their website.
4. Customers submit their name, phone number, enquiry message, and consent.
5. NexaFlow uses AI to understand the enquiry and identify which leads need faster follow-up.
6. The merchant opens a private inbox to see all enquiries.
7. NexaFlow prepares a suggested WhatsApp reply so the merchant can respond faster.
8. The merchant can track status, notes, follow-up date, and potential lead value.
9. After the trial, the merchant can choose a paid monthly plan if the product helps their business.

Mention the ecosystem:
NexaFlow is designed as one business core that can support Enquiry, CRM, Billing, Inventory, and Automation over time.

Avoid:
Do not mention APIs, token credits, model routing, OpenAI, OpenRouter, backend, database, webhooks, or infrastructure details.
Do not guarantee sales or revenue.
Do not say NexaFlow replaces the business owner.
Do not make unrealistic claims.

Suggested conversation:
Speaker A opens with the problem: small businesses miss WhatsApp enquiries and reply too late.
Speaker B asks what NexaFlow does.
Speaker A explains NexaFlow Enquiry and the simple enquiry link.
Speaker B asks how a merchant starts.
Speaker A explains the 30-day trial and setup steps.
Speaker B asks what happens when a customer submits an enquiry.
Speaker A explains AI sorting, reply drafts, and the private inbox.
Speaker B asks whether NexaFlow is only for enquiries.
Speaker A explains that Enquiry is the first product, and NexaFlow can grow into CRM, Billing, Inventory, and Automation.
Speaker B asks for the final benefit.
Speaker A ends with: Start with NexaFlow Enquiry, stop losing enquiries, and build a more organized business.

Please make the final output sound like a short premium SaaS explainer for local service merchants.
```
