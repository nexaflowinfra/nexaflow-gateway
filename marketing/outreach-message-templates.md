# NexaFlow Outreach Message Templates

These templates are for manual, respectful outreach to local service merchants. Personalize the first line before sending.

## Positioning

NexaFlow Enquiry helps local service merchants collect customer enquiries into one private inbox, prepare WhatsApp reply drafts, and track follow-ups so fewer leads are lost.

Best first offer:
- 1-month trial
- Simple setup
- No need for the merchant to build a full website
- Suitable for WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram, Google Business Profile, and simple enquiry links

## Suggested 7-Day Manual Outreach Flow

Day 1:
- Add 20 target merchants to `lead-outreach-tracker.csv`.
- Contact the top 5 highest-fit merchants.

Day 2:
- Contact another 5 merchants.
- Reply to any interested leads with a demo link.

Day 3:
- Review which industries replied.
- Add another 10 merchants from the best-performing industry.

Day 4:
- Follow up with Day 1 merchants who did not reply.

Day 5:
- Offer trial setup to interested merchants.
- Record `Trial offered` or `Trial started`.

Day 6:
- Contact another small batch of highly relevant merchants.

Day 7:
- Review results:
  - Reply rate
  - Trial interest
  - Common objections
  - Best-fit industry

## What To Track After Every Message

Update these fields in the CSV:
- `Status`
- `Last Contacted`
- `Next Follow-up`
- `Trial Interest`
- `Plan Interest`
- `Notes`

## Do Not Send

Avoid messages that:
- Promise guaranteed sales
- Claim full automation without limits
- Pressure the merchant
- Ask for customer data before trust is established
- Hide that this is a trial or new product
