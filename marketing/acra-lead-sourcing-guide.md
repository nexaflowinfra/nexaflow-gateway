# ACRA Lead Sourcing Guide

Purpose: use ACRA Bizfile as a compliant starting point to find Singapore businesses that may fit NexaFlow Enquiry. Do not scrape, bulk-message, or use private personal data. Use ACRA only to identify registered businesses and business activity fit, then verify public business contact channels from the company's own website, Google Business Profile, LinkedIn page, Facebook page, Instagram page, or other public business listings.

## What To Use

Use ACRA Bizfile's Industry search, not People search.

ACRA's Industry search can find live businesses by business activity, keyword, or SSIC code. Search results can show up to 250 matching results, and ACRA recommends narrowing results with one business activity or filters when too many matches appear.

Official references:
- https://www.acra.gov.sg/resources/guides-forms/using-bizfiles-search-functions/
- https://www.acra.gov.sg/resources/eservice-tools-portals/bizfile/

## Best Search Strategy

Start with industries where NexaFlow Enquiry has a clear pain point:

1. Renovation and interior design
2. Cleaning and home services
3. Repair and maintenance services
4. Beauty salons, wellness, and aesthetic services
5. Tuition, enrichment, and training providers
6. Event, bridal, photography, and local service consultants

Avoid starting with large enterprises, finance, law, medical providers with strict compliance workflows, or companies that already appear to have mature CRM or booking systems.

## Step-by-Step Workflow

1. Open ACRA Bizfile:
   https://www.bizfile.gov.sg/

2. Find the search area for business information.

3. Choose the `Industry` tab.

4. Search one activity keyword at a time, for example:
   - renovation
   - interior design
   - cleaning
   - repair
   - beauty
   - salon
   - tuition
   - training
   - event
   - photography

5. Select only one relevant business activity first. This keeps the result list focused.

6. Filter for live businesses if the filter is available.

7. For each promising company, record only:
   - business name
   - UEN if visible
   - ACRA status
   - business activity
   - entity type
   - ACRA source URL or note

8. Do not contact the company using personal officer details from paid reports. Instead, search the company name on:
   - Google
   - Google Maps / Google Business Profile
   - LinkedIn
   - Facebook
   - Instagram
   - the company's official website

9. Only add outreach contact details if they are clearly business-facing and publicly listed, such as:
   - official website contact form
   - official company email
   - official WhatsApp button
   - official Facebook or Instagram page
   - official LinkedIn company page

10. Score the lead before outreach.

## Fit Score

Score `5`:
- Service business with quotation, appointment, consultation, or follow-up needs
- Public WhatsApp or social messaging channel
- Active social page or Google Business Profile
- Owner-operated or small-team business
- No obvious CRM or booking automation

Score `4`:
- Likely receives enquiries, but the workflow is not fully visible
- Has public contact channels
- Good industry fit

Score `3`:
- Possible fit, but enquiry volume is unclear

Score `2`:
- Weak fit, inactive presence, or mostly walk-in only

Score `1`:
- No clear enquiry flow, no public business contact, too large, or already has mature systems

## Outreach Rule

Send one personal message only. If there is no reply, follow up once after 3-5 business days. If they decline, stop contacting them.

Do not send bulk messages. Do not imply an existing relationship. Do not claim that ACRA endorsed NexaFlow.

## First Batch Target

Build the first ACRA-sourced batch with 30 businesses:

- 6 renovation or interior design
- 6 beauty, wellness, or salon
- 5 cleaning or home service
- 5 repair or maintenance
- 4 tuition, enrichment, or training
- 4 events, bridal, photography, or consultants

After 30 leads, review:

- Which industry replied fastest
- Which pain point was easiest to explain
- Which channel worked best
- How many asked for price
- How many accepted a 1-month trial

Then focus the next batch on the top 1-2 industries.
