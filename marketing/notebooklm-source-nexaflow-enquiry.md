# NexaFlow Enquiry Product Brief for NotebookLM

## Product Name
NexaFlow Enquiry

## Simple Positioning
NexaFlow Enquiry is an AI WhatsApp enquiry assistant for local service businesses.

It helps merchants collect customer enquiries, classify the lead, prepare a WhatsApp reply draft, and manage follow-ups in a private merchant inbox.

## Target Customers
This product is mainly for local service businesses that depend on WhatsApp enquiries, including renovation businesses, tuition centres, beauty and wellness services, repair services, clinics, property agents, event services, and other appointment or quotation-based businesses.

## Problem
Many small businesses receive enquiries from WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram, Google Business Profile, and websites. The problem is that enquiries are easy to miss, hard to organize, and slow to follow up.

When a merchant replies late, the customer may already choose another provider. When the merchant has no clear inbox, leads stay scattered across chat history, DMs, and notes.

## Solution
NexaFlow gives the merchant a simple enquiry link and optional website widget. Customers submit their name, phone, message, and consent.

The system then:
- Stores the lead in a private merchant inbox.
- Classifies the enquiry by intent and priority.
- Estimates the potential lead value.
- Creates a suggested reply draft.
- Generates a WhatsApp follow-up link.
- Allows the merchant to update status, notes, follow-up date, and deal value.
- Keeps consent records for privacy and PDPA-style handling.

## Why Merchants Pay
Merchants are not paying for "AI" alone. They are paying for faster response, fewer missed enquiries, better follow-up, and a more professional customer experience.

The product is valuable because even one recovered customer can be worth more than the monthly subscription for many service businesses.

## Why It Is Easy to Adopt
The merchant does not need a full website or complicated CRM. They can share one enquiry link on WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram, Google Business Profile, or their existing website.

For merchants with a website, NexaFlow also provides an embed widget.

## Key Benefits
- Simple setup for non-technical merchants.
- AI reply draft for faster follow-up.
- Merchant inbox for all enquiries.
- Lead priority and intent classification.
- WhatsApp follow-up link.
- Follow-up date and internal notes.
- CSV export.
- Privacy consent record before form submission.

## Short Marketing Message
Stop losing WhatsApp enquiries. NexaFlow helps local service businesses capture leads, reply faster with AI, and manage every follow-up from one simple inbox.

## Call to Action
