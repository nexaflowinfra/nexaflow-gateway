# First Batch Lead Criteria

Use this checklist before adding a merchant to `lead-outreach-tracker.csv`.

## Ideal Customer Profile

Start with local service merchants who:
- Receive customer questions before closing a sale
- Need to reply quickly to win the customer
- Use WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram, Google Business Profile, or phone calls
- Have repeated enquiries, quotations, appointments, or follow-ups
- Do not already have a strong CRM system
- Are small enough to make decisions quickly

## Best Early Industries

Priority 1:
- Renovation and interior design
- Beauty salons and aesthetic clinics
- Cleaning services
- Tuition and enrichment centres
- Repair and maintenance services

Priority 2:
- Event planners
- Bridal services
- Photography and videography
- Pest control
- Car workshops
- Local consultants

Priority 3:
- Small clinics
- Pet grooming
- Fitness studios
- Home service providers
- Training providers

## Fit Score Guide

Score `5` if:
- They clearly use WhatsApp for sales enquiries.
- They offer services that need quotation or appointment follow-up.
- Their social page shows active customer comments or messages.
- Their business looks owner-operated or small-team.

Score `4` if:
- They likely receive enquiries, but their workflow is not fully visible.
- They have public contact details and active social presence.

Score `3` if:
- They might need it, but the enquiry volume is unclear.

Score `2` if:
- They are too small, inactive, or mostly walk-in only.

Score `1` if:
- There is no clear customer enquiry flow.
- They are a large company with existing systems.
- The contact detail looks private rather than business-facing.

## Good Signs

Look for:
- "WhatsApp us"
- "DM for quote"
- "Book appointment"
- "Message us for package"
- "Free consultation"
- "Send floor plan for quotation"
- "Limited slots"
- Slow or inconsistent social replies
- Many repeated questions in comments

## Weak Signs

Be careful with:
- Businesses with no public contact channel
- Large brands with corporate systems
- Pure ecommerce shops with direct checkout
- Pages that look inactive
- Personal accounts instead of business accounts

## First 20 Lead Mix

Recommended first test batch:
- 4 renovation or interior design businesses
- 4 beauty or aesthetic businesses
- 3 cleaning or home service businesses
- 3 tuition or enrichment businesses
- 3 repair or maintenance businesses
- 3 event, bridal, photography, or local consultant businesses

## Review After First 20

Measure:
- How many replied
- Which industry understood the product fastest
- Which pain point was strongest
- Whether they preferred English, Chinese, or both
- Whether they wanted WhatsApp demo, website demo, or trial account

Then focus the next 50 leads on the best-performing industry.
