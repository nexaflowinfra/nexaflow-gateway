# NotebookLM Video / Audio Script Under 60 Seconds

## Format
Two-person Q&A conversation.

## Estimated Length
Around 50 to 55 seconds.

## Script

**Host A:** Small businesses get enquiries every day on WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram, and Google. But many of those leads get lost or replied to too late.

**Host B:** So what does NexaFlow Enquiry actually do?

**Host A:** It gives a local service business one simple enquiry link. Customers submit their name, phone number, message, and consent.

**Host B:** And where does the AI come in?

**Host A:** NexaFlow classifies the enquiry, identifies priority, estimates potential value, and prepares a WhatsApp reply draft for the merchant.

**Host B:** Does the merchant need a website?

**Host A:** No. They can use the enquiry link directly. If they already have a website, they can also add the NexaFlow widget.

**Host B:** Who is this best for?

**Host A:** Renovation, tuition, beauty, repair, clinic, property, and other local service businesses that depend on fast follow-up.

**Host B:** So the main benefit is simple?

**Host A:** Yes. Fewer missed leads, faster replies, organized follow-up, and a more professional customer experience.

**Host B:** In one sentence?

**Host A:** NexaFlow Enquiry turns WhatsApp enquiries into organized business opportunities.

## Optional Ending Text
Try NexaFlow Enquiry: AI WhatsApp enquiry assistant for local service businesses.
