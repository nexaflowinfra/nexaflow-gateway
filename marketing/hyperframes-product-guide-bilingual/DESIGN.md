# NexaFlow Product Guide Bilingual Design

## Style Prompt

Dark premium AI business walkthrough for local service merchants. The video should feel like the actual NexaFlow product page and merchant inbox: practical, clean, confident, and easy to follow. Use bilingual Chinese and English captions, cursor-guided steps, restrained motion, and security callouts that make merchants trust the system.

## Colors

- Background: `#030504`
- Panel: `#0c0f0e`
- Strong panel: `#060808`
- Text: `#f8faf7`
- Muted text: `#b9b3a5`
- Teal accent: `#2dd4bf`
- Gold accent: `#f3c76a`
- Safe green: `#22c55e`
- Warning red: `#ff6b6b`

## Typography

- Primary: `Inter`, `Segoe UI`, `Arial`, sans-serif
- Chinese fallback: `Microsoft YaHei`, `Noto Sans SC`, sans-serif

## Motion

- Cursor-led walkthrough with clear pauses after each action.
- Cards and browser panels should slide in gently and stay readable.
- Use teal/gold glow sparingly around important clicks and security moments.
- Keep subtitles large, high contrast, and away from UI details.

## What NOT To Do

- Do not make it look like an API developer tutorial.
- Do not overcrowd the screen with too many dashboard rows.
- Do not hide security/privacy points in tiny footnotes.
- Do not use jump cuts between major steps.
- Do not overlap subtitles, cursor, and UI buttons.
