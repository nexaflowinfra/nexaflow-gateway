# NexaFlow Bilingual Product Guide Video

## Purpose

Short bilingual guide for merchants. It explains how to use NexaFlow Enquiry from product page to trial request, private inbox, customer link sharing, lead follow-up, and data protection.

## Suggested Caption

中文：
NexaFlow 帮本地服务型商家把客户询盘、WhatsApp 跟进和 lead 状态集中在一个 private inbox。一个链接给客户，一个后台给商家，跟进不再乱。

English:
NexaFlow helps local service merchants manage customer enquiries, WhatsApp follow-ups, and lead status in one private inbox. One customer link, one merchant workspace, cleaner follow-up.

Try NexaFlow Enquiry:
https://api.nexaflowinfra.com/ai-enquiry

## Key Talking Points

- One customer enquiry link.
- One private merchant inbox.
- WhatsApp-ready follow-up drafts.
- Lead statuses: new, contacted, quoted, won, lost.
- Data protection: consent, private access key, retention, audit log.
- Start with Enquiry, then add CRM, Billing, Inventory, and Automation later.
